| Newton's First-order integrals | (1) Linear Momentum: 
$$
\mathbf{F}_{i}=\frac{d\mathbf{p}_{i}}{dt}\hspace{1in}\int_{1}^{2}\mathbf{F}_{i}dt=\int_{1}^{2}\frac{d\mathbf{p}_{i}}{dt}dt=\left( \mathbf{p}_{2}- \mathbf{p}_{1}\right) _{i} \nonumber
$$
 (2) Angular momentum: 
$$
\frac{d\mathbf{L}_{i}}{dt}=\mathbf{r}_{i}\times \frac{d\mathbf{p}_{i}}{dt}= \mathbf{N}_{i}\hspace{1in}\int_{1}^{2}\mathbf{N}_{i}dt=\int_{1}^{2}\frac{d \mathbf{L}_{i}}{dt}dt=\left( \mathbf{L}_{2}-\mathbf{L}_{1}\right) _{i} \nonumber
$$
 (3) Kinetic energy: Thus the differential, and corresponding first integral, form of the kinetic energy can be written as 
$$
\mathbf{F}_{i}=\frac{dT_{i}}{d\mathbf{r}_{i}}\hspace{1in}\int_{1}^{2}\mathbf{F}_{i}\cdot d\mathbf{r}_{i}=(T_{2}-T_{1})_{i} \nonumber
$$
