# Variational Principles in Classical Mechanics

*Third edition* by [Douglas Cline](http://classicalmechanics.lib.rochester.edu/) (University of Rochester).

This MyST edition is a web-native conversion of the open textbook hosted at the
University of Rochester River Campus Libraries, using the structured
[LibreTexts](https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Classical_Mechanics/Variational_Principles_in_Classical_Mechanics_(Cline)) edition for text and mathematics and the
author PDF for reference.
